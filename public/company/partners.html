Get into the graph.

LocalTour's business graph is curated, not scraped. Every venue in a city has been reasoned into place — category, neighborhood, concierge weight, and why it earned its slot. That scarcity is the whole value.

If you run a business in one of our eight cities (or a city we're spinning up next), there are two doors: the curated door, and the partner door. The curated door is how you get picked by the concierge. The partner door is how you earn placement, boost affiliate priority, and show up in trip planner threads.

Questions, answered.

FAQ
01

How is this different from Yelp, TripAdvisor, or a city blog?

We're curated, not scraped; contextual, not star-rated; and commission-aware without being commission-captured. The concierge is a named local with a node tree, not a review aggregator. Shorter answer: we're a decision engine, they're a directory.

02

What cities are you building next?

We're actively scoping Las Vegas, Nashville, Austin, Denver, Phoenix, Salt Lake City, Kansas City, and Portland. If your city is on that list, we want to hear from you yesterday. If it isn't, tell us why it should be.

03

Do you take equity or cash?

For most partnerships, cash or revenue share. For city partnerships with strong strategic fit (a flagship city with deep tourism infrastructure), we'll consider structured equity arrangements. We don't take creator equity — that's just commission.

04

Can I partner with you and SH@W Labs across multiple products?

Yes, and several of our partners already do. SH@W Labs runs 17 products — if your brand touches hospitality, legal, work, or lifestyle, there's probably more than one surface we can build on together. Start with LocalTour and we'll route the rest.

05

Who owns the content created for a partnership?

You own the content you contribute. We own the platform and our editorial additions. Community Wall posts remain the property of their authors under our content license. Detailed IP terms are in the Partner Agreement — we send it before any work begins.

06

Is there a minimum commitment?

Merchants and creators: no minimum. City partnerships: 12 months minimum because the build work and the compounding only make sense at that horizon. Month-to-month hosting extensions are available after the initial term.
