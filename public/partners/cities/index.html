From raw city to live engine.

The pipeline · 4 stages
STAGE 01

Intelligence

We ingest the raw signal — points of interest, review corpora, event feeds, seasonal rhythm, and the local knowledge only your bureau holds — and reason it into structured facts about your city.

STAGE 02

Graph

Every venue is reasoned into place: category, neighborhood, concierge weight, and the why behind its slot. Curated, never scraped. The scarcity is the whole value.

STAGE 03

Persona

A named local concierge is grown on top of the graph — a node tree with taste and point of view, not a chatbot bolted onto a search box. It answers like someone who lives there.

STAGE 04

Ship

The config-driven city site goes live in production: concierge, community wall, trip planner, coupon clipper, and a scroll cinematic. Your domain, ready for traffic.

Economics · Pick your structure
Option A

Flat build & host

A fixed fee to build your city and a predictable monthly to host it. Clean line item for a budget cycle, no revenue accounting, no surprises. You know the number before we start.

Option B

Revenue share

Little to nothing upfront. We share revenue against affiliate and merchant placement in your city. Our incentives point the same way yours do — a city that actually converts.

Option C

Hybrid

A reduced build fee plus a smaller share. The middle path most DMOs land on — some certainty for finance, real upside as the city compounds. Structure follows your budget cycle.

Exit clause

You own your data. Merchant lists, content, event calendars, community posts — all exportable. If the partnership ever ends, you leave with everything except the LocalTour tech stack itself. No lock-in, no hostage data. Twelve-month minimum on city builds, because the compounding only makes sense at that horizon.
