How it stays real.

Wall architecture
01

Share in seconds

No account wall. No karma score. One form, five post types — tip, event, photo, question, shoutout. Done in under 20 seconds.

02

Verified lightly

We check you're in the city, not who you are. Local IP, light rate-limit, and active moderation — no legal-ID-required friction.

03

Surfaces by signal

The concierge reads the wall. Good tips get cited in itineraries. Events get pinned. Dead posts fade. Real contribution gets real reach.

What it's not.

Positioning
Review-site era

Star ratings. Ghost reviews. SEO sludge.

  • Businesses gaming stars for visibility
  • Reviewers writing for status, not for you
  • Feed sorted by ad spend, not signal
  • Locals long since stopped posting there
  • Question → paragraph of copy-paste
The Wall

Real signal. Real locals. Now.

  • No stars. Just tips, events, and asks.
  • Locals post because the concierge cites them
  • Feed sorted by freshness and upvote velocity
  • Moderation is a feature, not an afterthought
  • Question → local answers in minutes
