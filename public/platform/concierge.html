What makes it different
Contextual

A recommendation for brunch on a rainy Tuesday isn't the same as a Saturday at noon. The concierge accounts for it without being asked.

Curated

No scraped long-tail. The business graph is reasoned, weighted, and tied to the concierge's node tree. Fewer picks, sharper picks.

Commission-aware

The concierge knows which merchants are affiliate partners — but that never beats a better answer. A good rec, first. Revenue, second.

Always on

No hours. No queue. No tipping. Ask the question you were about to Google, and get the answer a local would give.
